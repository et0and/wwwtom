# About

Hi, I'm Tom,

I am a design engineer with a background in the arts and education. Presently I am working as a developer in Pōneke, Te Whanganui-a-Tara. Prior to this, I taught design, art and digital technology at [Takapuna Grammar School](https://takapuna.school.nz), where I also worked on the development of Te Wāhi Auaha (a school maker space and innovation space).

My experience in making learning more accessible and equitable across a range of students from different backgrounds and abilities built the foundation of my human-centered design practice.

Previously I studied at the [Elam School of Fine Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elam_School_of_Fine_Arts) where I also briefly taught the first and second year studio programme.

Find me on [Are.na](https://are.na/tom), [Read.cv](https://cv.tom.so/), and [Merveilles](https://merveilles.town/@tomupom). Sometimes, I am on IRC as @tomupom on Libera Chat and Rizon networks. You can also find me on Urbit as ~worbur-dorneb.

I have accounts on other popular social media sites, but rarely use them.
